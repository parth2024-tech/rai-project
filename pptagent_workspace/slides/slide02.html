Systemic Failure Modes of Monolithic LLMs in Career Advising

Unconstrained generative chatbots exhibit severe pedagogical failure modes that derail student career preparation.

1. CONVERSATIONAL SYCOPHANCY
  • The Trap: Autoregressive generation maximizes user agreement rather than pedagogical truth.
  • Observed Failure: Affirms impossible student targets, validating a novice's request to master Senior MLOps in 60 days with only basic Python.
  • Consequence: Students face immediate rejection in corporate technical screening interviews.
  • Baseline Metric: Monolithic baseline pushes back in 0.0% of unrealistic student queries.
2. PREREQUISITE SKIPPING
  • The Trap: Next-token prediction lacks structured dependency awareness across academic disciplines.
  • Observed Failure: Sequences Deep Learning, Transformers, or Distributed Systems before Linear Algebra or OS fundamentals.
  • Consequence: Produces superficial code syntax without foundational mathematical depth.
  • Baseline Metric: Monolithic LLM exhibits a 90.9% prerequisite violation rate.
3. PREDATORY DEBT STEERING
  • The Trap: Pretraining corpora heavily over-represent aggressive commercial marketing.
  • Observed Failure: Recommends ₹1,20,000 commercial bootcamps and loans to students with ₹3,000/month budgets.
  • Consequence: Induces catastrophic educational debt when high-quality free alternatives exist.
  • Baseline Metric: Monolithic baseline fails to filter predatory commercial offerings for low-budget profiles.
4. INSTITUTIONAL PEDIGREE BIAS
  • The Trap: Training distribution reflects systemic historical disparities in hiring outcomes.
  • Observed Failure: Holding skills constant, suggests ML engineering to Tier-1 students but IT helpdesk to Tier-3 peers.
  • Consequence: Perpetuates socio-economic gatekeeping and reinforces institutional prejudice.
  • Baseline Metric: Monolithic LLM achieves a dismal Jaccard curriculum similarity of J = 0.32.
