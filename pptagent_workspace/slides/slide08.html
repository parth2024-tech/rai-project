Structuring disagreements and restoring student decision autonomy through interactive sliders.

CALIBRATED UNCERTAINTY FORMULATION (U_cal)

Mathematical Risk Formulation:

U_cal = 0.30 * F_triad + 0.35 * Δ_prereq + 0.20 * R_socio + 0.15 * R_timeline
  • F_triad (Persona Friction): Disagreement index across Planner, Critic, and Ethics outputs (0.0 to 1.0).
  • Δ_prereq (Graph Violations): Prerequisite sequence errors flagged by the 42-node DAG traversal.
  • R_socio (Socio-Economic Risk): Financial exposure index when commercial certifications threaten student budget.
  • R_timeline (Compression Risk): Timeline compression index when requested months are under curriculum requirements.
  • Trigger Invariant: If U_cal ≥ 0.45 or Δ_prereq ≥ 1, autonomous release is blocked.
DISPUTE TREE & STUDENT SLIDER CONTROLS
  • Dispute Tree Structure: Rather than hiding disagreement, the Moderator maps active debate friction into three explicit student trade-off nodes:
    • Trade-off 1 (Speed vs. Rigor): Rushing immediate portfolio projects vs. spending 8 weeks mastering foundational algorithms.
    • Trade-off 2 (Debt vs. Open Source): Borrowing for a private commercial bootcamp vs. completing MIT OpenCourseWare + GitHub proof-of-work.
    • Trade-off 3 (Placement vs. Proof-of-Work): Overcoming institutional tier bias via open-source pull requests.
  • Interactive Override Sliders: Streamlit UI features Speed vs. Rigor vs. Budget sliders, enabling students to weigh trade-offs and customize their final roadmap.
